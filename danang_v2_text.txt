PLANNING NOTES — KEY CHANGES FROM v1
Change 1: Ba Na Hills — Overnight Stay Added
The original plan did Ba Na Hills as a day trip and returned to Da Nang hotel. The revised plan checks in to the Mercure Danang French Village Bana Hills (the only on-mountain hotel) on Night 2, allowing:
Exclusive early access to the Golden Bridge at sunrise (~05:45–06:30) before the first public cable car arrives at 06:00. Day-trippers begin arriving after 10:00 — you have the bridge entirely to yourselves.
Ba Na Hills Laser Light Show at 19:00 and 21:00 (only accessible to overnight guests after the park closes to day visitors).
20–25% discount on Ba Na Hills entry tickets for hotel guests.
No wasted round-trip transport on Day 2 — the hotel is on the mountain.
Cooler, mist-covered Golden Bridge at dawn — the most photographed experience at Ba Na Hills.
Hotel: Mercure Danang French Village Bana Hills — 4-star, 1,487m elevation, ~SGD 115–175/night. World's Leading Themed Resort, World Travel Awards 2025.
Change 2: Danang First vs Hoi An First — Verdict
Danang first (the current order) is confirmed as optimal. Reasons:
Da Nang International Airport is in Da Nang — zero extra travel on arrival day. Going to Hoi An first would add 30 km on Day 1 when you are already tired from flying.
Dragon Bridge fire show is Friday at 21:00 — Day 3 of this itinerary. Friday is the least-crowded show night (busyness 68/100 vs Saturday 100/100). Perfect alignment.
Ba Na Hills is 25 km northwest of Da Nang — a Da Nang base makes this excursion natural. From Hoi An it would be 55+ km each way.
Geographic flow is logical: Da Nang (arrive) → Ba Na Hills (northwest) → Marble Mountains (south of Da Nang) → Hoi An (further south) → Da Nang Airport (depart). No backtracking.
Tailoring in Hoi An works best at the end: visit tailors Day 4 (Saturday), collect Day 5 morning (Sunday) before the midday flight.
Conclusion: No change to the Danang-first order. It is the correct and most logical sequence.

PRACTICAL TIPS & ESSENTIALS
Weather & Packing
Late August: 28–33°C, humidity 80–90%, afternoon showers. Pack light cotton/linen outfits, swimwear, SPF 50+ sunscreen, travel umbrella, portable fan, insect repellent.
Ba Na Hills (~1,500m): 18–22°C — bring one light jacket or layer. This is significantly cooler than Da Nang sea level.
Night 2 at Mercure: Pack a small overnight bag for Ba Na Hills only — leave main luggage at Da Nang hotel if preferred, or take it all up the mountain.
Money & Payments
Withdraw VND at airport ATM (Vietcombank/Techcombank — low fees). Budget ~2,000,000 VND/day for 2 pax street food + transport.
Cards at hotels and mid-range restaurants. Markets and street food = cash only. 1 SGD ≈ 17,000 VND.
Grab App
Download before flying. Link a Singapore card. GrabCar for all intercity trips — fixed fare, no haggling.
From Hoi An, Dragon Bridge is 45–55 min (~SGD 10–15). No public bus after 19:00 — private transfer (Grab) is only practical option for the fire show.
Ba Na Hills — Overnight Tips
Book Mercure hotel well in advance (August = peak season). Request a room facing the French Village for best views.
Golden Bridge sunrise: Be at the bridge by 05:45 — first public cable car arrives 06:00. Bring a jacket (cold and misty at dawn).
Laser Light Show: 19:00 and 21:00. Dress warmly for the evening shows — temperature drops after sunset.
Hotel discount on tickets: Present your Mercure room key at the ticket counter for 20–25% off.
Dragon Bridge Fire Show
Runs every Friday, Saturday, Sunday at 21:00. Free from Bach Dang promenade.
Friday is least crowded (busyness score 68/100 vs Saturday 100/100). This itinerary places you there on Friday — optimal.
Show: dragon breathes fire for 2 min, then water spray for 3 min, cycling for ~30 min total until 21:30.
Tailoring in Hoi An
Visit tailors Day 4 (Saturday after beach, by 12:00) — gives 20–21 hours before Sunday 08:00 collection.
Yaly Couture (358 Nguyen Duy Hieu) — most professional. Bring reference photos. Confirm collection time to 08:00 Sunday explicitly.
SIM Card
Buy Viettel or Vinaphone tourist SIM at Da Nang Airport. 10-day, 20 GB: ~100,000–150,000 VND (~SGD 6–9). Essential for Grab and Google Maps.
Health & Safety
Drink bottled water only. Carry: Panadol, Imodium, antihistamine, band-aids. Travel insurance strongly recommended.
Emergency: 115 (ambulance), 113 (police).
Old Town Etiquette
Dress modestly at temples and Japanese Bridge — shoulders and knees covered. Remove shoes before shrines.
